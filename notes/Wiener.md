# Norbert Wiener


## Concepts
- Ontogenetic learning vs. phylogenetic learning
	- Ontogenetic learning: Learning occuring at the scale of an individual during its lifetime
	- Phylogenetic learning: Learning occuring at the scale of a collective (family, culture, species) during its evolutionary timeline
