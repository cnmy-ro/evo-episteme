# Notes on Other Writers


## Spinoza

Concepts:
- Conatus: persistence, existential inertia
- Theory of knowledge
- Theory of affects, the concept of time, and the perceived realness of things
- Isomorphy between mental and physical aspects of the world
- God/Nature as the sole existing substance/object/thing
- Nature of Evil as a simple absence of truth (i.e. an error) without any positive existence of its own

Concepts:

## Goethe

Concepts:
- Urphänomen (Primal Phenomenon)
- Exact sensory imagination: "This requires the observer to internalize the form of an organism and mentally track its unfolding transformations. Rather than detaching the mind from nature through cold intellect, Goethe believed the human mind is part of nature's very act of self-revelation."
- Theory of colors
